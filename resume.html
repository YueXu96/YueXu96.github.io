Resume

Yue is an ARB Registered Architect and RIBA Chartered Member. She joined Bjarke Ingels Group in London in November 2021, where she has spent four and a half years across office, residential and religious buildings.

Before that she trained at Hunan University and the Bartlett; in practice her work runs from early massing and planning submissions through to end‑of‑trip fit‑out and façade detailing.

Her work moves between the two ends of practice: the technical discipline of stage 3–4 delivery — BIM coordination, product selection, consultant liaison — and the earlier‑stage curiosity that shaped her student years, when parametric pavilions, UAV photography and computational mapping were as much a part of the toolkit as the drawing board.
